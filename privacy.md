# ERG Ridest Privacy Policy

Effective: October 22, 2026  
Controller: Julio Patricio Contreras Marchant, Viña del Mar, Chile · Contact: patrick_01_pato@outlook.com

## Summary

ERG Ridest has no ads, analytics or tracking, and you never sign up. Your rides, workouts, settings and health data stay on your iPhone (and your Apple Watch, if you use it): we never receive them.

The one thing our server keeps is your diamonds, the Garage's paid currency: so they can't be forged and you keep them. It keeps them under a random ID that isn't your name, your email or your Apple Account (see Diamonds and your account).

## What the app stores and where

| Data | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Your settings (FTP, weight, max heart rate, zones, preferences) | To work out targets and zones | Only on your iPhone |
| Rides, workouts, calendar and plan | Your history and progress | Only on your iPhone |
| Saved Bluetooth devices (identifier and name) | To reconnect your trainer and sensors | Only on your iPhone |
| Scrap, medals and what's worn in the Garage | The Garage | Only on your iPhone |
| Diamonds (see below) | So they can't be forged, and you keep them | On our server |

## Diamonds and your account

The first time you get diamonds (a gold or platinum medal, or a purchase), the app makes an account on our server with a random ID. If you never get any, no account is made. The server keeps:

- the random ID, and a code that lets your iPhone open the account;
- each diamond in and out: what it was (a medal level, a bundle bought, an item bought or a repaint), how many and when;
- for each bundle you buy, its App Store transaction number, so the server can ask Apple to confirm it was bought for your account, and hear from Apple if it's refunded;
- the items you bought with diamonds;
- only if you sign in with Apple: the ID Apple gives our app for you (not your name or email: the app doesn't ask for them) and the token used to revoke the sign-in when you delete the account.

It never receives your rides, power, heart rate, health data, FTP, weight, location, name, email or contacts. To keep diamonds yours when you change iPhone, the account's code is saved in your iCloud Keychain, which is end-to-end encrypted, and Sign in with Apple is optional.

Our server runs on Cloudflare, which processes these data on our behalf only to run it, and also sees your connection's IP address to reply to your iPhone. We don't record IP addresses. We use these data only to keep your diamonds (legal basis: to provide the service you asked for), never for advertising, and we don't share them with anyone else. They're kept until you delete the account.

You can delete the account in Settings › Account › Delete account: everything above is deleted from our server at once, including diamonds you bought, and Sign in with Apple is revoked. Settings › Start over keeps the diamonds you bought with money and deletes the rest. You can also write to us to get a copy of these data or to have them corrected or deleted.

## Apple Health (HealthKit)

Only if you turn on Settings › Save to Health:

- The app **writes** each ride measured by a real trainer or power meter to Health: the indoor cycling workout, energy calculated from your watts, distance, power, cadence and heart rate.
- It **reads** your weight, only if you ask to use it in Settings.
- On Apple Watch, the app reads your heart rate during the ride to show it on the iPhone; that Watch workout is discarded, not saved.

Health data never leaves your devices, is never shared with anyone, and is never used for advertising or for anything other than showing and saving your workouts. You can revoke access in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › ERG Ridest, and delete what the app saved from the Health app.

## Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth only to connect to your smart trainer, power meter, cadence sensor and heart-rate monitor. It doesn't use Bluetooth to locate you or to detect other devices for any other purpose.

## Notifications

Session reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No notifications are sent from a server.

## Backups

If you create a backup, it's a file you save wherever you choose (for example Files or iCloud Drive). You manage that file; we have no access to it. Diamonds aren't restored from a backup: they're on our server.

## Email support

If you tap Settings › Contact support, an email to us opens. Below your message it carries a code of your settings (your choices in the app, without your FTP, weight or max heart rate), the app and iOS versions, the iPhone model and the language. You see it before sending, you can delete it, and it only reaches us if you send it. We use that email only to answer you.

## Third parties

The app includes no third-party SDKs, ads, tracking or analytics. Apple handles the purchase, the download and Sign in with Apple under its own privacy policy. Cloudflare runs our server as described above.

## Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13. Beyond the diamonds' random account above, it collects no one's data.

## Deleting your data

Settings › Start over erases everything the app stores on the iPhone; you can also delete the app. Settings › Account › Delete account deletes what our server keeps. What was saved to Health is deleted from the Health app.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here with its date.
